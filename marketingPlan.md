## What we sell (and why the pitch writes itself)

Every lead we scan gives us *concrete numbers*:
- Load time in seconds
- SSL expiry date
- Broken links count
- Missing analytics / pixels
- No DMARC → emails landing in spam

That data IS the cold opener. No generic pitch needed.

## Channel Plan

### 🟢 Free / Near-free

#### Google Business Profile
- Create or claim at business.google.com
- Fill 100%: hours, photos, services, website
- Ask every past client for a review
- Local searches for `"web agency [city]"` show GBP first
- **Effort:** 2h setup, 30min/week to respond to reviews
- **Expected:** 3–8 inbound leads/month at zero cost

#### Reddit + Facebook Groups
- Target groups: `"small business owners [country]"`, `"ecommerce [city]"`, `"restaurant owners [country]"`
- **Do not pitch.** Answer real questions about slow sites, SSL errors, Google ranking drops
- End every answer: *"We run free website audits at levelcoding.com if you want to see your numbers"*
- One good answer = 10+ DMs
- **Effort:** 1h/week

#### LinkedIn Organic Posts (weekly)
- Post one scan finding per week using real data from the CRM scanner
- Example formats:
  - *"Scanned 50 barbershop websites in Tallinn — 78% have no HTTPS. Here's what that costs them."*
  - *"3 SSL certificates expiring this week among our leads. Each one = full-screen browser warning for visitors."*
  - *"Restaurant websites: avg load time 6.8s. Google's threshold is 3s. No wonder nobody finds them."*
- Screenshot the worst findings (blur company name)
- No budget needed, compounds over time
- **Effort:** 2h/week
- **✅ CRM already generates this data automatically**

#### Cold Email (already running)
- Highest ROI B2B channel with a concrete problem/solution
- **✅ Fully built in LeadManager — warmup, templates, tracking**

### 🟡 Cheap (€20–100/month)

#### Google Ads — Exact Match Only
- Start budget: **€5/day**
- Target keywords with buying intent only:
  - `"website too slow"`
  - `"fix ssl certificate"`
  - `"wordpress speed optimization [city]"`
  - `"website not showing on google"`
  - `"ssl expired website"`
- Run for 2 weeks, kill any keyword with 0 conversions
- **Expected:** 2–5 inbound leads/month at €50–100 spend

#### SEO Blog Posts (Google Search Console)
Each post targets one scan finding — low competition, long-tail, ranks in 4–8 weeks:

| Post title | Target keyword | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Why your SSL certificate kills customers | `ssl certificate kills sales` | Low |
| Your site loads in 7 seconds — here's why Google ignores you | `website too slow google ranking` | Low |
| No DMARC record: why your emails land in spam | `business email going to spam` | Low |
| What is an H1 tag and why does it matter for SEO | `h1 tag seo` | Medium |
| Facebook Pixel vs Google Analytics: which do you need | `facebook pixel or google analytics` | Medium |

- Write 1 post/week, 800–1200 words, real examples from scanner data
- Submit to Google Search Console after publishing
- **Effort:** 3h/post, free traffic forever after

#### YouTube Shorts / TikTok
- 30–60 second screen recordings, no face required
- Format: *"I scanned 100 [niche] websites — here's what I found"* → show the dashboard
- Shows authority + builds trust at scale
- **Effort:** 2h/week

### 🔵 Medium Effort, High Return

#### Partner with Local Accountants / Business Consultants
- They already meet small business owners monthly
- Pitch: *"Refer us a client who needs a website fix — we pay 20% of invoice"*
- One active partner = 5 clients/month
- Find them on LinkedIn, local chambers of commerce
- **Effort:** 1 day to set up 5 partnerships

#### Upwork / Fiverr — "Website Audit" Gig
- List at **€49** per audit
- You run the scanner, export the report, deliver in 24h
- Clients who pay for an audit convert to full projects at high rate
- Builds reviews and social proof
- **Effort:** 2h setup, 30min per gig delivery
- **✅ CRM Full Report already generates this report**

#### Cold LinkedIn DMs
- 3–5x higher response rate than cold email for B2B
- **✅ LeadManager now has a "LinkedIn" button per lead row** — opens Google pre-search:
  `site:linkedin.com/in "Company Name" "City" owner OR founder OR manager`
- Opener uses scanner data directly:
  > *"Hi [Name], ran a quick audit on [domain] — loads in 6.2s on mobile (Google's limit is 3s), no DMARC so emails likely land in spam, no Meta Pixel so you can't retarget visitors. Happy to fix all three in one afternoon — want the full report?"*
- Numbers come from **Copy Report** button (one click from warmup grid)
- **Daily limit:** 20 connection requests + 10 DMs on free LinkedIn
