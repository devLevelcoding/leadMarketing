# Mini CRM Industry Analysis

Based on live lead database (35,675 total leads). Excludes LMS and ShopLibrary.

---

## Tier 1 — Easiest to build + most leads

| Industry | Leads | Domain | Why it's easy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Beauty / Hair / Nail / Barber** | ~4,750 | crm | Appointments + client history + loyalty. One repeating loop. |
| **Gym / Fitness / Personal Trainer** | ~3,400 | crm + health | Memberships + renewal reminders + attendance. Very predictable. |
| **Auto Repair Shop** | ~1,275 | crm | Vehicle history + service reminders. No competitor owns small shops. |
| **Dentist / Dental Clinic** | ~1,350 | health | Patient recall + appointment reminders = almost sells itself. |

> These 4 alone = **10,000+ warm leads** already in the DB. Each has a simple, repeating client workflow that maps to the same 3 screens: client list → appointment → follow-up.

## Tier 3 — High value, more complex to build

| Industry | Leads | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Lawyer / Law Firm** | ~1,100 | Client intake + case status. High willingness to pay ($100+/mo). |
| **Accountant / Accounting Firm** | ~637 | Clients + tax deadlines + documents. Very repetitive workflow. |
| **Travel Agency / Tourism** | ~2,900 | Booking management + follow-ups. Biggest domain by lead count. |
| **Architect / Architecture Firm** | ~750 | Project milestones + client follow-ups. Medium complexity. |

---

## Strategic Recommendation

The **lowest-effort, highest-impact** next CRM after LMS/ShopLibrary:

1. **Beauty Salon CRM** — 4,750 leads, trivial workflow (book → remind → rebooking), no-shows are a universal pain, $30-50/mo price point is uncontested at local level.
2. **Auto Repair CRM** — underserved niche, sticky (vehicle history keeps clients locked in), service reminder = recurring revenue hook.
3. **Veterinary CRM** — almost zero competition for small clinics, pet vaccination reminders are genuinely urgent to owners.

The beauty/hair/nail cluster alone justifies a single product since all three share the same core: **client → appointment → reminder → rebooking.**
