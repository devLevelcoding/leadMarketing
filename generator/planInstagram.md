# 60-Day Instagram Plan — IT Company Launch
> New page, experienced company. Build trust first. Sell second.

---

## The Rule Before Day 1

Nobody buys from a new Instagram page. They buy from people they trust.
The first 30 days you **never sell** — you prove you know what you're doing.
Days 31–60 you sell, but the trust is already there.

---

## Phase 1 — Days 1–30: Build the Foundation
**Goal:** Establish identity, show expertise, attract the right followers

### Week 1 — Introduction (Days 1–7)

| Day | Post | Format | What it does |
|-----|------|--------|--------------|
| 1 | "Who we are" — company intro, what you build, who you serve | Static / Reel | First impression |
| 2 | Your founder story — why you started, years of experience | Personal photo + caption | Human connection |
| 3 | The 3 problems your clients always come to you with | Carousel | Speaks to pain points |
| 4 | Your tech stack — what tools/languages you use and why | Visual grid or carousel | Signals expertise |
| 5 | A day in your work life — desk, screen, process | Reel / BTS | Makes you real |
| 6 | "What most people get wrong about hiring an IT company" | Static or carousel | Controversy = reach |
| 7 | Stories only — poll, question box, reaction | Story | Engagement warmup |

### Week 2 — Expertise Signals (Days 8–14)

| Day | Post | Format |
|-----|------|--------|
| 8 | "5 signs your website is killing your business" | Carousel |
| 9 | Behind the scenes of building something (no client name needed) | Reel |
| 10 | Industry stat + your take ("73% of SMBs have no analytics — here's why it matters") | Static |
| 11 | Tool of the week you actually use | Static + caption |
| 12 | "The most common mistake businesses make with their website" | Carousel |
| 13 | Introduce a team member or your work setup | Photo |
| 14 | Stories: share a meme, poll on dev tools | Story |

### Week 3 — Education Heavy (Days 15–21)

| Day | Post |
|-----|------|
| 15 | How-to tutorial — "How to check if your site loads in under 3 seconds" |
| 16 | "What is [concept] — explained for non-technical people" (SEO, CRM, API, automation) |
| 17 | Before/after — a website or system you improved (even generic/mockup) |
| 18 | "3 things to do before you hire any developer" |
| 19 | Reel: your morning routine or coding process |
| 20 | "Why your business needs automation in 2025" |
| 21 | Stories: Q&A — let them ask tech questions |

### Week 4 — Social Proof (Days 22–30)

| Day | Post |
|-----|------|
| 22 | Case study #1 — anonymized ("Client X had problem Y, we built Z, result was W") |
| 23 | Numbers post — years of experience, projects delivered, countries served |
| 24 | Testimonial (screenshot or graphic — even from LinkedIn/email) |
| 25 | "How we work" — your process in 5 steps |
| 26 | FAQ — questions clients always ask before hiring you |
| 27 | Hot take: "You don't need a mobile app — you need this instead" |
| 28 | Stories: share the case study again, more reach |
| 29 | Portfolio piece — screenshot + story of what you built |
| 30 | Milestone post — "30 days on Instagram, here's what we learned" |

## Phase 2 — Days 31–60: Sell (With Dignity)
**Goal:** Convert warm followers into inquiries. Trust is built — now make offers.

### Week 5 — Services Introduction (Days 31–37)

| Day | Post |
|-----|------|
| 31 | Services overview — what you offer, who it's for (clear, no jargon) |
| 32 | Service deep-dive #1 — "What's included when you hire us for web development" |
| 33 | "How much does a professional website cost?" — demystify pricing, show value |
| 34 | Service deep-dive #2 — automation, CRM, lead gen tools |
| 35 | ROI post — "A slow website loses you X. Here's what fixing it costs vs. earns" |
| 36 | "Who is this for" — describe your ideal client so clearly they recognize themselves |
| 37 | Stories: DM CTA — "Want a free audit?" |

### Week 6 — Objections & Authority (Days 38–44)

| Day | Post |
|-----|------|
| 38 | "We already have a website" — why that's not enough |
| 39 | "We can't afford it" — reframe cost as investment |
| 40 | "We'll do it in-house" — when that works and when it doesn't |
| 41 | Trend post — AI, automation, what's changing in your space |
| 42 | "Myths about IT companies debunked" |
| 43 | Case study #2 — different industry, different problem |
| 44 | Stories: poll — "What's your biggest tech headache?" |

### Week 7 — Direct Selling (Days 45–51)

| Day | Post |
|-----|------|
| 45 | Clear offer post — "We build X for Y — book a free call" |
| 46 | "What happens in our first meeting" — remove the fear of reaching out |
| 47 | Problem → Agitate → Solve post |
| 48 | Limited offer — free audit, free strategy call, free review |
| 49 | Testimonial #2 + CTA |
| 50 | "What you get when you work with us" — tangible deliverables list |
| 51 | Stories: countdown to offer deadline |

### Week 8 — Trust + Urgency (Days 52–60)

| Day | Post |
|-----|------|
| 52 | Personal story — a project that almost failed and what you learned |
| 53 | "Questions to ask before hiring any IT company" (you answer them all) |
| 54 | Result reel — screen recording of something you built |
| 55 | Soft educational post + CTA at the end |
| 56 | "We only take X clients per month" — scarcity if true |
| 57 | Collaboration or partnership shoutout |
| 58 | Community post — "What do you want us to build next?" |
| 59 | Tease — "Something big coming next week" |
| 60 | 60-day milestone + what you offer + genuine CTA |

## Content Format Mix (every week)

| Format | Frequency | Why |
|--------|-----------|-----|
| **Carousel** | 2–3x/week | Most saves, most reach |
| **Reel** | 1–2x/week | Discovery, new followers |
| **Static** | 1x/week | Quick, fills the grid |
| **Stories** | Daily | Keeps you top of mind |

## Posting Schedule & Optimal Times

### Best Time Per Post Type

| Post type | Best time | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Carousel** | Wed / Thu 18:30 | After work, highest saves & shares |
| **Reel** | Tue / Thu 19:00 | Peak discovery window, algorithm pushes hard |
| **Static** | Mon / Fri 08:00 | Quick morning scroll, low competition |
| **Story** | Daily 08:00 + 20:00 | Twice a day keeps you top of mind |
| **Engagement post** (poll, question) | Sun 19:00 | Relaxed evening, people interact more |

**Best days:** Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday consistently outperform weekends for service businesses.

---

### Recommended Weekly Schedule

| Day | Content type | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Story | 08:00 |
| Wednesday | Post (carousel / single) | 18:30 |
| Friday | Story | 08:00 |
| Sunday | Post (engagement bait) | 19:00 |

> 4 touches/week is the sweet spot — enough to stay in the feed without burning out content.

---

### For Local Service Audiences (Beauty / Fitness / Restaurants)

Target audience is mostly **women 25–45** checking Instagram in the evening.
- Peak: **Wednesday & Thursday 18:00–21:00**
- Schedule feed posts at **18:30**, stories at **08:00**
