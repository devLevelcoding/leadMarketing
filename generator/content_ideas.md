# 20 Content Ideas — IT Company Promotion
> Practical, funny, useful. No fluff.

---

## Reels (screen record — zero design needed, max reach)

**3. "We fixed this in 4 minutes. The client waited 3 years."**
Screen record of fixing a broken contact form.
Caption: *"They thought it was working the whole time."*

**7. "Your website in 2019 vs your competitor's in 2025"**
Before/after mockup. No words needed. Just the images.

**13. "Your website's load time vs your competitor's"**
Open PageSpeed Insights live, type in a local business URL, show the score.
No names needed — people recognize themselves.

**17. "Speed test live — 5 local businesses"**
Open GTmetrix, test 5 real local businesses in your city. React to each score.
People tag their own businesses.

**11. "We asked ChatGPT to build a website. Here's what happened."**
Show AI output vs real professional result. Funny + positions you as the human that makes it work.

**19. "The 30-second Instagram bio audit"**
Pull up a random business Instagram, check 5 things live:
link in bio, contact, highlights, last post date, profile photo quality.

## Carousels (save-worthy, high reach)

**1. "The €0 website audit"**
Checklist of 10 things anyone can check on their own site in 5 minutes.
Title: *"We charge €500 for this. Here it is for free."*

**2. "Is your website older than your phone?"**
Avg phone replaced every 2 years. Avg website replaced every 7.
Side-by-side: 2018 site vs 2025 site.

**6. "We translated your IT invoice"**
Left column = what the agency wrote. Right column = what it actually means.
Funny + builds trust.

**9. "What €500/month buys in digital tools"**
List 8–10 tools (CRM, email, analytics, automation) — all free or under €20/mo.
Title: *"You don't need a big budget. You need the right stack."*

**10. "Red flags when hiring a web developer"**
Each red flag = one slide.
*"No portfolio? Run. 'It'll be ready in 2 weeks'? Run faster."*

**14. "Why your Facebook page is not a website"**
Debunk the "we have Facebook, we don't need a website" myth.
Include stats on what happens when Facebook goes down.

**18. "We built a full CRM in a weekend. Here's what it took."**
Hours, tools, cost breakdown.
Ends: *"Off-the-shelf CRM: €200/month. Custom built: one-time €X."*

**20. "What your invoice actually pays for"**
Pie chart of a €1,500 website project: design, dev, testing, hosting, revisions, support.
Title: *"You're not paying for code. You're paying for 0 headaches."*

## Static posts (quick to make, good for engagement)

**4. "Google your own business right now"**
Just that sentence. Then:
*"If you're not on page 1 for your city + service, your competitor is. DM us."*

**8. "The 3am email that saved a client €12,000"**
Story of catching a server crash before the client noticed.
*"This is why we have monitoring."*

**12. "The most expensive word in tech"**
Big text: **LATER.**
Caption: *"'We'll fix the website later.' Later costs more than now. Every time."*

**16. "The silent salesman"**
*"Your website works 24/7. Never calls in sick. Doesn't ask for a raise.
Is yours actually selling anything?"*

---

## Stories (manual post, interactive)

**5. "The €3 coffee that costs businesses €3000"**
*"Every day your slow website loses visitors = money left on the table.
Fix it for the price of one month's coffee budget."*

**15. "What happens when you don't renew your domain"**
Screenshot of a real expired domain being squatted.
*"Someone bought their domain for €12. Now selling it back for €800."*
