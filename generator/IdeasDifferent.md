## FREE TOOLS (build on your site — leads come to you)

**1. Website Grader** — enter URL → score 0–100 across speed, security, SEO, tracking, mobile.
You already have the scan engine. Email capture to see full report. Best ROI tool you can build.

**2. GDPR Compliance Checker** — check if site has cookie banner, privacy policy, HTTPS, contact page.
EU businesses are scared of fines. Fear converts. Output: "3 GDPR issues found."

**3. Business Email Health Checker** — enter domain → check SPF, DKIM, DMARC.
Most small businesses fail all 3. Their emails land in spam. You already have this in LeadReport.

**4. Privacy Policy Generator** — form: company name + country + email → generates GDPR policy.
Everyone needs this. Insane conversion rate. You get their email.

**5. Page Speed Checker** — enter URL → show load time, score, what's slow.
Uses PageSpeed Insights API (free). Simple to build. Highly shareable.

**6. Mobile Friendly Checker** — check viewport, font size, touch targets.
Half of small business sites fail this. Instant lead opener.

**7. Local SEO Scorecard** — check Google My Business completeness, NAP consistency.
Local businesses obsess over this. Direct service upsell.

**8. Free Invoice Template** — Google Docs link, branded lightly.
Post in groups. People share tools. Passive traffic forever.

**9. Free CRM Template (Google Sheets)** — simple lead tracker, contact log.
Freelancers and small businesses need this. Post everywhere.

**10. QR Code Generator** — enter URL → download QR code for business card/menu.
Restaurant owners love this. Zero effort to build. High shareability.

## CONTENT (post this, people follow and eventually buy)

**11. "Before/after" posts** — show a real site you improved. No client name needed.
The visual contrast does the selling for you.

**12. "5 signs your website is losing you customers"** — carousel or reel.
Pain-point content gets saved and shared more than anything else.

**13. "What a €500 website vs. €5000 website actually looks like"** — comparison post.
Destroys the "we'll go cheap" objection before they even ask.

**14. Screen recording reels** — record yourself fixing something live.
Real work = real credibility. No editing needed.

**15. "Client asked us to…" story posts** — anonymized, relatable.
Human storytelling > corporate announcements every time.

**16. Hot take posts** — "You don't need a mobile app. You need this instead."
Controversy gets comments. Comments get reach.

**17. Tool of the week** — show one tool you actually use and why.
Educational content builds authority without selling.

**18. "What I wish clients knew before hiring a dev"** — honest, useful.
Gets shared by both clients and other devs.

**19. Industry stat + your take** — "73% of SMBs have no analytics. Here's why."
Easy to write. Positions you as someone who reads and thinks.

**20. FAQ posts** — questions clients always ask before hiring you.
Answers objections before the sales call. Works as evergreen content.

## OUTREACH (reach them before they find you)

**21. UK/DE agency subcontracting emails** — email small agencies (2–10 people) offering white-label dev.
They have clients, they hate hiring. One relationship = years of work.

**22. Personalized cold email to no-website leads** — you have 25,000 leads in the scraper.
Filter: no website + has phone + Germany/Netherlands. Email in their language.

**23. LinkedIn connection + value post** — connect, wait 2 days, share one useful post. No pitch.
Relationship first. Ask never comes if the content does the work.

**24. Instagram warm DM** — comment genuinely for 3–4 days, then DM with a specific observation.
"Noticed your booking link goes to a Facebook page — that costs you 40% of mobile visitors."

**25. Reply to "looking for developer" posts on LinkedIn** — search the phrase weekly.
These are warm leads posting in public. First reply wins most of the time.

**26. Answer questions in Facebook groups** — even if you hate Facebook, clients are there.
Lurk in local business owner groups. Answer tech questions. Never pitch.

**27. Telegram entrepreneur groups** — "antreprenori", "afaceri romania", "startup romania".
Romanian groups for Romanian leads. Western groups for Western leads.

**28. Reddit r/entrepreneur + r/smallbusiness** — answer questions honestly.
English audience, global reach. Link to your tool when relevant (not spam).

**29. Partner with graphic designers** — they get clients who need websites.
Offer 10–15% referral. One partner = 5+ clients/year. No cold outreach needed.

**30. Partner with accountants and lawyers** — they advise businesses who need websites.
Especially new businesses who just registered. Perfect timing.

## PARTNERSHIPS & NETWORK

**41. Join a local coworking space** — one conversation = one client. Proximity converts.

**42. Speak at a local business event** — even 15 minutes on "why your website loses clients."
Positions you as the expert in the room immediately.

**43. Offer a free workshop** — "How to check if your website is costing you customers."
20 people in a room. 3 become clients.

**44. University/accelerator partnerships** — startups need cheap, good dev work.
You get portfolio pieces. They get affordable quality.

**45. Connect with business coaches** — they advise SMBs who always need a developer.
Same referral logic as accountants. One coach = ongoing pipeline.

## LONG GAME (takes months but compounds forever)

**46. Write one article/month** — "How we cut a restaurant website load time from 8s to 1.2s."
Google indexes it. Ranks for "web developer [city]" over time. Free SEO.

**47. Open source a small tool on GitHub** — a useful snippet, a template, a CLI tool.
Developers find it, star it, some become clients or referrers.

**48. YouTube — screen recording tutorials** — "How I audit a website in 10 minutes."
YouTube is the second biggest search engine. Long-tail searches bring leads for years.

**49. Build a niche product** — a simple SaaS for one industry (restaurants, dentists, salons).
€29/month × 50 clients = €1,450 MRR. Recurring revenue, not project work.

**50. Email newsletter** — one useful tip per week to past clients and leads.
Keeps you top of mind. When they need work, they think of you first.
