# LevelCoding — Daily 1h Marketing Routine
> Updated: 2026-04-28

---

## 1h/day routine

| Time | Task | Tool |
|---|---|---|
| 15 min | Send today's warmup batch, mark sent/skip | LeadManager |
| 20 min | LinkedIn: find 5 profiles → send 5 DMs | LinkedIn button → Copy Report |
| 10 min | Answer 1 question on Reddit or Facebook group | Browser |
| 10 min | Leave 2 comments on LinkedIn posts from target audience | LinkedIn feed |
| 5 min | Reply to any replies/DMs from yesterday | Email + LinkedIn |

Consistent beats intense.

## Free channels not yet used

### Google Maps Reviews
- Ask every client to leave a Google Business Profile review
- 5 reviews puts you above 90% of local IT agencies
- Add to email signature: *"Happy with our work? Leave us a Google review — 30 seconds."*

### Email Signature
Every email is a free impression. Use:
```
LevelCoding · Web performance & SEO fixes
Free website audit → levelcoding.com/audit
```

### Local Business Directories (free listings, 1h total)
- Clutch.co — IT agencies, clients search here
- Sortlist.com
- GoodFirms.co
- Your country's local business registry
List once, stays forever.

### LinkedIn Comments (not posts)
- Find posts by business coaches, accountants, marketing consultants with 50+ likes
- Leave a smart 2-line comment — their audience sees you
- Costs nothing, scales fast

### Cold Calls
Underrated in 2026 because everyone switched to email. Call the same leads in your CRM.
- 30 calls/day = 3–5 real conversations
- Opener: *"I ran a free audit on your website and found something I wanted to flag — do you have 2 minutes?"*

### WhatsApp Broadcast
- Already built in LeadManager
- For phone-only leads (no website) this is the only channel

### Nextdoor / Local Facebook Groups
- Join groups for your city's business owners
- Answer questions, don't pitch
- End with: *"Hi, local web developer here — happy to help"*

### Partner Outreach (one email, 10 accountants)
```
Subject: Free value-add for your clients

Hi [Name],

I run LevelCoding, we fix websites for small businesses.
I'd like to offer your clients a free audit as a value-add from you —
if any of them convert, we split 20%.

Want to try it with one client?

Marian — LevelCoding
```

```
- [No DMARC / SSL expires in X days / X broken links]

Happy to fix all of this in one afternoon.
Want me to send you the full report?

Marian — LevelCoding
```
Numbers come from the **Copy Report** button in LeadManager (one click).

## The Compounding Stack (free, builds over time)

```
Week 1–4  : LinkedIn DMs + warmup emails daily
Month 2   : Google Business Profile + directory listings done
Month 2–3 : 1 blog post/week from scanner data
Month 3+  : Blog posts start ranking, inbound begins
Month 4+  : 1 partner sending referrals/month
Month 6+  : LinkedIn audience grows, posts get reach without ads
```

Cold outreach (email + LinkedIn) → clients **now**
Content (blog + LinkedIn posts) → clients **in 3 months**
Both together → no dry months.

## Why the scanner is the unfair advantage

No other local agency walks into a conversation knowing:
- The prospect's exact load time in seconds
- SSL certificate expiry date
- That their emails are landing in spam (no DMARC)
- How many broken links are on their homepage
- Which social platforms they're missing

That data turns a cold message into a warm one.
